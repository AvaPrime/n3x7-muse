# PRD — N3X7 / MUSE Music Intelligence Workbench v0.1

## Problem

Generative music makes creation cheap but creates a new bottleneck: understanding, taste, curation, provenance, version management, and meaningful creative direction.

N3X7 has 1,000+ existing generations that are difficult to organize. The product must turn a chaotic generation archive into a living creative system.

## Product thesis

**We are not building a music generator. We are building a system for music understanding, taste, inspiration, curation, and sonic engineering.**

## Primary user

N3X7 artist/producer as the proving-ground user.

## v0.1 goals

1. Establish the canonical domain vocabulary.
2. Make the library intelligible through multidimensional music representations.
3. Make prompts and production-direction artifacts reusable.
4. Preserve track/version/provenance lineage.
5. Surface taste patterns without replacing human judgement.
6. Establish provider-neutral adapter boundaries.
7. Provide an executable UI that can evolve into the Artist OS.

## Non-goals

- Direct Suno scraping or reverse-engineered API dependence.
- Autonomous publishing.
- Autonomous artist identity decisions.
- Payments.
- Public SaaS tenancy.
- Biometric collection.
- Full audio DSP implementation in the UI scaffold.

## Future hypotheses

- Audio → CreativeGenome can materially improve curation.
- Taste models can improve discovery and generation direction.
- Album Genome can produce more coherent releases than isolated track generation.
- Quality gates can reduce AI-music “slop”.
- Provenance can become a valuable product surface.
